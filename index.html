What's inside

Everything a hostel office actually does, in one place

No modules to configure and no features you'll never touch — just the day-to-day work of running a hostel, made faster.

01
Student records
One profile per resident with contact details, emergency contacts and academic info — searchable in seconds instead of buried in registers.
02
Room allocation
See vacant beds by floor and block at a glance, and assign incoming students without cross-checking three spreadsheets.
03
Gate pass & security
Students request outings, wardens approve them, and security confirms entry and exit against the same record — no paper slips, no guesswork.
04
Maintenance requests
Residents log electrical, plumbing or repair issues directly from their room, and staff can track what's open, in progress or resolved.
05
Notice board
Mess timings, rule changes and urgent alerts reach every resident's dashboard the moment they're posted.

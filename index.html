One Platform. Complete Management.

Designed to simplify everyday hostel operations.

Hostel Management System is a web-based application developed to bring essential hostel operations into one centralized platform. It provides tools for student management, room allocation, outing requests and administration.

Core Features

Everything organized in one place.

01

Student Management

Manage student information and hostel-related records through a centralized system.

02

Room Management

Organize blocks, rooms, occupancy and student room allocation efficiently.

03

Outing Requests

Students can submit outing requests while administrators manage approvals.

04

Administration

Centralized tools help administrators manage important hostel activities.

05

Authentication

Account-based access keeps student and administrative areas separated.

06

Digital Workflow

Replace repetitive manual processes with a structured digital workflow.
